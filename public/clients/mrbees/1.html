The Estate

A Grand Tradition
Reborn in Bronze

Nestled within seventeen acres of working meadowland on the North Downs, Mr. Bee's Brewery has been a quiet custodian of English countryside brewing for over three decades. What began as a single copper kettle in a converted grain barn has grown into one of the most respected small-batch breweries in the South East.

The Philosophy

Geometric Craft,
Organic Soul

Every batch is governed by the same principle: precision in method, wildness in spirit. We draw water from an on-site chalk spring, source honey from our own hives at the wood's edge, and harvest hops from neighbouring estates where they have grown since the reign of Edward VII.

The Provenance Field to Firkin

Every ingredient carries a postcode. Every flavour, a story rooted in Kent's ancient landscape.

01

The Hive

Raw Wildflower Honey

Harvested in late August from six Langstroth hives at the meadow's edge, where clover, borage and bramble flower in perpetual succession.

02

The Hop Garden

East Kent Goldings

Grown on a neighbouring estate in Faversham under the same family for four generations. Resinous, floral, and irreducibly English.

03

The Spring

Chalk-Filtered Water

Drawn from a subterranean chalk aquifer beneath the estate. Naturally mineral, exquisitely soft — the hidden architecture of every brew.

04

The Malt House

Maris Otter Barley

Malted at Crisp Malting in Great Ryburgh, using the oldest commercial barley variety in continuous production. Its biscuit warmth is unmistakable.

The Method From Grain to Glass

I

Milling

Grist prepared at dawn in the Victorian mill house.

II

Mashing

Chalk spring water meets grain at precisely 67°C for ninety minutes.

III

Kettle & Honey

Wildflower honey folded into the boil at flame-out, never before.

IV

Fermentation

Open-topped vessels, heritage yeast strain maintained since 1994.

V

Conditioning

Cask-conditioned in the cool chalk cellar for no fewer than twenty-eight days.
