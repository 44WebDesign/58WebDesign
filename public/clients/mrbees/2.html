The Craft

Malting
Floor-malted British barley from traditional Yorkshire maltings.

Brewing
Copper kettle, open mash. Local honey added late in the boil.
III

Oak conditioning vessels. Slow, cool, patient. We never rush the yeast.

Our Provenance

Where Every
Pint Begins

Mr. Bees was never planned. It started with too much honey, a second-hand homebrew kit, and a long winter with nothing better to do. The first batch was rough. The second was better. By the fifth, people were asking for more.

Every beer we make still starts with honey from local hives — not as a gimmick, not for the label, but because it genuinely makes a better pint.

"The bees do the hardest part.
We just don't get in the way."
