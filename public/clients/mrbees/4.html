Our Sacred Ritual

A Century of the Swarm

Founded in 1894 by Silas "Mr. Bees" Beauregard, our brewery began as a modest apiary on the outskirts of the city. Silas believed that the secret to a superior ale lay not just in the grain, but in the alchemy of the nectar gathered by his loyal swarms.

Today, we maintain the same Victorian methods. We harvest our own heirloom honey, fire our kettles with coal-fed determination, and age our barrels in the very vaults where Silas first struck his first cask. We do not rush the process; we brew with the steady, rhythmic pulse of the hive.

Each batch is a tribute to our heritage—a connection to the land, the bees, and the craftsmanship of a bygone era that we refuse to let fade into history.
