The Neighborhood Story

Built For The
City Crowd.

Ricky’s Bread & Beyond began as a tiny after-hours sandwich counter hidden inside a converted warehouse district. Word spread through musicians, chefs, students, and late-shift locals searching for comfort food with real craft behind it.

Today, the shop still carries that underground spirit — sizzling grills, butcher paper wraps, warm light against exposed concrete, and recipes layered with smoky, indulgent flavor that tastes like the city itself.
