Our Story

Food Made
By Someone
Who Cares

Ricky started this place with a griddle, a notebook full of recipes, and a conviction that a great sandwich is a small act of love. Seven years later, nothing's changed — except the queue out the door.

Every bun is baked fresh. Every patty is hand-formed and pressed to order. The sauces are made in-house, the produce comes from suppliers Ricky knows by name, and no two days taste exactly the same.

This isn't a franchise. It's a place. Come in, grab a stool, and stay a while.
