Our Story

Based in Felixstowe. Trusted across Europe.

Founded on the belief that extraordinary vehicles deserve extraordinary care, Platinum Sports & Prestige has become the partner of choice for discerning clients across the UK and mainland Europe.

Our approach is simple: treat every vehicle as if it were irreplaceable, because to its owner, it is. From the moment we collect your car to the moment it is delivered, you receive complete transparency, total security, and genuine expertise.

Whether you're transporting a race car to a circuit, a classic to a concours, or a supercar to a second home in Europe, we are the specialists you want behind the wheel of your logistics.
