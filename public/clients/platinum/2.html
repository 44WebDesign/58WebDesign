Who We Are

Specialists in Bespoke Vehicle Transport

Based in Felixstowe, Platinum Sports & Prestige was built on a singular conviction: that the most extraordinary vehicles in the world deserve transport that matches their calibre. We handle each commission personally.

Whether you're moving a track-day Ferrari, a restored classic, or a fleet of prestige SUVs across Europe — we treat every vehicle as if it were our own. Full insurance, GPS tracking, climate-controlled enclosed trailers.
